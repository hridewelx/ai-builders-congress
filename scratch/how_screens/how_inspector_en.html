How it works
1. AI proposes

An unsupervised autoencoder learns what clean knit texture looks like from pixels alone. It proposes candidate bounding boxes and a class guess. It has no severity head, no count, no verdict, and no way to approve or reject a lot.

2. Humans confirm

A human reviewer confirms, edits, rejects or adds each defect, selects the zone, and reviews every view. Severity is looked up strictly from the buyer clause list after human confirmation — never from the model.

3. The rule decides

A deterministic pure function compares confirmed defective-garment counts against entered Ac/Re limits in strict precedence: Plan → REJECT → ESCALATE → HOLD → ACCEPT. Same inputs always produce the exact same outcome.

Honest limits

  • Uncalibrated detector: scores are raw reconstruction errors, not probabilities.
  • Trained and evaluated on staged knit panels only; no factory floor evidence yet.
  • Type classification is a heuristic suggestion; the human reviewer can change it freely.
  • Critical non-visual defects (broken needle, metal fragments) cannot be photographed; a Supervisor records them separately.
  • Sampling randomness is self-declared. Software cannot physically verify the draw.
  • No ISO certification, no "ISO-compliant AI", and sampling tables are not reinvented from the standard.

Design principles

Explainability

Every box includes a reconstruction-error attention map and numeric score. The decision page shows exact reason codes, counts, limits, and a SHA-256 hash of the confirmed-defect snapshot.

Data sovereignty

Inference runs locally server-side (CPU, in-process), never through a third-party cloud API. In the production topology, photos never leave the factory network and the vision model has no external network access.

Fail-safe by construction

Verified across 12 automated unit tests: failed jobs, missing files, unmapped clauses, pending critical checks, or missing sampling plans strictly produce HOLD or ESCALATE, never ACCEPT. Rather than an automated blur filter, human reviewers mark blurry/unusable photos for retake, which immediately holds the lot until recaptured.

Inspection Terminology & Glossary ▾
Ac / Re (Acceptance / Rejection Number): Ac is the maximum number of defective garments permitted to accept the lot; Re is the minimum number of defectives that causes lot rejection.
AQL (Acceptable Quality Limit): The maximum percent defective considered satisfactory as a process average under the buyer sampling standard.
ESCALATE vs HOLD: ESCALATE flags conditions requiring human policy judgment (unmapped buyer clauses, reviewer disagreements); HOLD flags incomplete evidence (missing photos, unreviewed views, missing sampling plan).
SHA-256 Snapshot Hash: A 256-bit cryptographic fingerprint computed over all confirmed defects and inspection parameters, locking evidence immutability upon decision computation.
