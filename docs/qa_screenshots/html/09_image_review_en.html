System Configuration & Catalog

Enterprise Synchronization (Read-Only):
Products, buyers, and defect severity clauses are synchronized from the enterprise ERP. In Version 1, specifications are strictly read-only on inspection terminals to preserve compliance integrity (FR-CLS-001).

Defect Classification Standard

Severity is server-assigned based on defect type, garment zone (1: chest/front, 2: sleeves/back, 3: hem/collar), and buyer clauses. The 7 defect types are fixed per PRD FR-REV-002.
