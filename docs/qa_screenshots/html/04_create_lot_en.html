What happens next

  1. 1
    Sampling plan A Supervisor configures sample size n and per-class Ac/Re limits. Without an active plan the lot remains on HOLD.
  2. 2
    Capture Inspectors register garments and upload front and back photos using phone cameras.
  3. 3
    Review Inspectors review on-device AI proposals. Every proposal requires human verification; blank reviews are never an automatic pass.
  4. 4
    Decision The deterministic decision engine evaluates confirmed defect counts against the configured plan limits.
